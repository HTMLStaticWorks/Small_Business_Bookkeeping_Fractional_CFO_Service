TIMELINE ARCHITECTURE

Inside our 5-day monthly close process

While typical firms take 20 to 30 days to return messy numbers, VALENCE adheres to an institutional close schedule.

DAY 01

Data Lockdown

Automated ingestion synchronizes all bank feeds, credit card charges, Stripe payouts, and payroll ledgers through the last calendar day.

DAYS 02–03

Ledger Reconciliation

Your dedicated senior bookkeeper categorizes line items, reconciles accounts receivable against payouts, and checks for uncategorized expenses.

DAY 04

CPA Review & Variance

Senior Controller inspects journal entries, applies accrual adjustments, and conducts budget vs actual variance analysis.

DAY 05

Statements Delivered

Final GAAP Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and cash runway forecast are published to your client portal, accompanied by an executive briefing.
